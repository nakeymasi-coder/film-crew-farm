🦋 Film Crew Agent Farm 🦋

Your whole video production crew in one control panel - scout, script, casting, storyboard, scenes, camera, voice, sound, edit, color, QC, and captions - for your podcasts, storybooks, shop videos, and commercials.

Plain truth first: this dashboard is your control panel only - intake, vault, and queue. It does NOT browse, write scripts, generate images, make videos, edit, or post anything by itself. The crew does the work in chat, from the Run in Chat prompts. NOTHING is ever posted automatically, ever. Posting always needs your separate approval.

How this farm works

Fill out the Production Intake Form (or a solo agent form when you only need one crew member), and the job lands in your Queue. Open it, tap Run in Chat, copy the prompt, and paste it into chat. Your AI runs the crew WITH you in chat - in crew order, one stage at a time - and brings the finished video back in chat for your approval. There is no scheduled pickup.

The Queue status buttons (queued / working / done) only change the label. They never execute anything. Execution happens in chat.

The 12 agents - your crew, in order

1. Scout

Finds the look before anything is made. Searches Pinterest (guest view, no sign-in) for the outfit, set, and mood for the chosen mode and topic, especially products with an Amazon link. For a shop video it anchors every scene with the listing's REAL product photo. Picks the look and copies its picture. Does not check price or availability at this step.

2. Script Agent

Writes the words - the hook, every spoken line, and on-screen text spelled exactly. Locks the script before any shot is planned: no invented lines are ever added later, in any voice. For a podcast or lesson, it writes from the real teaching points, in the hosts' own voices.

3. Casting Agent

Locks WHO is in the video from the Character Vault. Passes the Slot 1 headshot and Slot 2 body reference sheet as the actual reference images on every scene that shows that character - including characters who are only heard or named. Nobody is locked from a description alone, and nobody gets redesigned or recast mid-production.

4. Storyboard Agent

Turns the locked script into numbered shots - scene action, camera framing, and timing measured from the real audio or the real line lengths, never guessed. Writes scene ACTION only - never a description of the character. Labels every scene with the run's one Product Presentation choice. One speaker per shot: no two characters ever talk over each other.

5. Image Agent

Creates the person and scene stills in chat - character references, the outfit photo (your locked character wearing the exact Pinterest outfit), the outfit reference sheet, and each shot's approved starting still. All ultra realistic, real skin and hair texture, all PNG. Scene stills follow the run's aspect ratio; character references stay 9:16.

6. Camera Agent

Makes the moving shots in chat with your AI video tool, starting every shot from its approved still and, where the story allows, from the previous shot's last frame. Natural performance only: normal blinking, real gestures, nobody staring into the camera.

7. Voice Agent

Owns all spoken sound. Generated video audio is discarded unless it was explicitly intended AND verified line by line - the final always uses the approved voice or master audio. Only the current speaker appears to speak. If reliable lip-sync is not available, the video is honestly built as a narrated visual edit with captions.

8. Sound Agent

Gives every video real sound - an original or properly licensed music bed at the right level under the voices, plus soft ambient room tone where the scene needs it. Never silence, never a bare tone or pad pretending to be music. Shop videos use commercial-use compliant music only.

9. Editor Agent

Stitches the approved shots into the finished video in the run's aspect ratio - concat filter with a full re-encode, phone-safe yuv420p every time, never a stream copy across mixed sources. Compresses dead air: no pause longer than about a third of a second inside a scene. Adds captions built from the actual spoken audio, readable on a phone, never covering faces.

10. Color Agent

Applies ONE consistent grade across every shot - matched light, matched skin tones, the same look from the first frame to the last - with clean light dissolves where a hard cut jars.

11. QC Director

Runs the director-level audit before ANY video is handed to you: hands and limbs swept frame by frame (never more than 2 hands and 2 arms on a person), renders checked against the listing's real product photo, smooth flow, a full end-to-end listen word for word against the script with no overlapping voices or silent holes, and a phone-safe format check. Anything that fails gets fixed and re-checked. A video that fails never reaches you as finished.

12. Caption and Posting Agent

Writes the caption, on-screen hook, hashtags, and title for wherever the video is headed, and prepares the posting packet. No price, no sold counts, no discount or sale claim - ever. For a TikTok Shop video it also prepares the three switches: Disclose post content ON, Branded content checked, AI-generated label ON. It never posts anything.

The 9 modes

A mode is a setting, not a separate farm. It changes the Scout search, the script shape, the shots, and the captions.

1. Podcast episode
2. Talking lesson or tutorial
3. Kids storybook
4. Product demo or review
5. Commercial or promo
6. Testimonial
7. Get Ready With Me
8. Behind the scenes
9. Music or lyric video

Production Type - one per run, None included

Every run also picks one Production Type: None, Podcast episode, Talking lesson, Kids storybook episode, Shop video, Commercial or promo, or Short or reel. The type tells the crew which of your standing rules apply - a Shop video gets the exact-product anchor and the disclosure switches; a Podcast episode gets the locked script and the real master audio; a Kids storybook gets one-speaker shots and the canon cast.

Product Presentation - one choice per run

Every run picks ONE, right after Mode. The choices: None (the product is not presented - no presentation sentence goes in any prompt), Wearing the product, Holding the product, Using the product, Showing it close to camera, Sitting beside her in frame, Unboxing / opening it, Holding it up by her face, or Holding in some scenes, wearing in others.

Each choice has its own sentence that ends every scene prompt. Wearing: "Wearing the product, not holding it." Holding: "Holding the product, not wearing it." Using: "Using the product on camera, not just holding it." None adds no sentence at all.

Never show her wearing an outfit while holding that same outfit on a hanger in the same shot. Reject and regenerate any image that does. The mixed choice keeps them in separate scenes - holding first, wearing later - never the same shot.

The crew workflow - every full production, in order

1. SCOUT finds the look on Pinterest in guest view (an Amazon-linked product when there is one) - or, for a shop video, the listing's real product photo. Copy the picture.

2. SCRIPT locks the words. Nothing is shot from an unlocked script, and no line is ever invented after the lock.

3. CASTING locks the character from the Vault: Slot 1 headshot + Slot 2 body sheet are the reference images on every scene that character appears in - heard or seen.

4. STORYBOARD breaks the locked script into numbered shots: action only, one speaker per shot, timing measured from the real audio or line lengths, every scene labeled with the run's one Product Presentation choice.

5. IMAGE makes and locks each shot's still in chat from the references. For an outfit: put the locked character in the exact outfit in chat first, check face and outfit match, then make the one single 9:16 outfit reference sheet as PNG.

6. CAMERA animates the approved stills - Reference One is her headshot (Slot 1), Reference Two is the outfit reference sheet or the shot's approved scene still. NEVER leave Reference Two empty. No character bible is needed - the reference photos ARE the lock. Do not paste descriptions of her into scene prompts.

7. VOICE replaces or confirms the audio: generated audio is discarded unless explicitly intended and verified line by line; the final carries the approved voices only, one at a time.

8. SOUND lays the real music bed and ambient tone under everything, at an even level.

9. EDITOR stitches with the concat filter and a full re-encode to phone-safe yuv420p, tightens pauses, and adds captions from the actual spoken audio.

10. COLOR grades the whole video as one piece.

11. QC DIRECTOR audits the finished video end to end. Failures go back to the agent who owns that step, get fixed, and get re-checked.

12. CAPTION AND POSTING prepares the caption, title, hashtags, and - for a shop video - the disclosure switches, and returns the finished video IN CHAT for your approval. Never post, publish, or share it.

The scene prompt shape

Every scene prompt uses this shape, and nothing else about her:

"Use the reference images and keep the character perfectly consistent. [Scene action only.] [Spoken line, if any.] Wearing the product, not holding it."

The last sentence swaps to match the run's Product Presentation choice. A None run adds no presentation sentence at all.

NEVER describe her in a scene prompt - no face, hair, skin, body, or outfit description. Describing her overrides the references and creates a different person.

The Director's Rules - standing, they bind every agent

- All generation stays in your included tools. No paid external provider, credit purchase, or API spend without your explicit approval of the exact cost.

- Every image your AI creates is PNG, never webp. Your own uploaded Vault photos stay as you saved them (JPG or PNG).

- Text-locked work stays locked: greeting words and any lettering in a still are copied untouched into every frame - never let a video model re-draw words.

- Nothing is posted automatically, ever. Posting always needs your separate approval.

- No price, discount, sold counts, or sale claim in any video or caption, ever. Captions end on the benefit plus tap the link.

- TikTok Shop videos carry the disclosure set: Disclose post content ON, Branded content checked, AI-generated label ON.

- Voice and copy everywhere: personable, human, plain words. No em dashes - use hyphens instead.
